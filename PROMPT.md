# The prompt

This is the entire prompt for the lead agent.

```
I want you to build a Street Fighter-style 1v1 fighter with a roster of the current top ten richest people, each with unique movesets that reflect their idiosyncrasies and backgrounds. It should look and play like a real arcade fighter.

The quality bar is the local folder of real Street Fighter II stills covering character select, versus splash, in-fight dual health bars + timer + names, a special-move projectile in flight, and KO / You Win. Compare your work to those stills the same way Claude of Duty compared itself to real Call of Duty screenshots.

Divide into the smallest independently judged pieces. For each important piece, fan out a builder and a separate critic with fresh context. Each critic must inspect the real output, compare it directly with the bar — using a blind A/B comparison when possible — identify the biggest remaining gap, and send it back for another round. Keep looping until our output wins or I stop the run.

Maintain a simple live progress page that shows the work evolving over time.

Use subagents and ultracode.
```
